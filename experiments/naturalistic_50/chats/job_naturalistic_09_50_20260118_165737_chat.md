2.  **Negative Space:** This is the area *around* and *between* the subjects. It’s the background,

space to improve the overall composition.

*   **Defines the Subject:** The shape of the negative space is what creates the silhouette and

*   **Creates Balance:** An image crammed with "stuff" (too much positive space) can feel chaotic
and overwhelming. Negative space provides "breathing room," giving the viewer's eyes a place to rest
and making the composition feel more balanced and harmonious.
*   **Guides the Viewer's Eye:** The shapes formed by negative space can create invisible lines that
direct your gaze toward the focal point of the artwork.
*   **Evokes Emotion:** A large amount of negative space can create a sense of calm, openness, or

is called a "figure-ground reversal," and it proves that the negative space is just as active and
important as the positive space.
